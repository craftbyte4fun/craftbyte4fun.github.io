Stick Slam Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026 · Developer: CraftByte4Fun · Contact: craftbyte4fun@gmail.com

Stick Slam ("the game") is a single-player game. This policy explains what information is handled when you play it.

Which version this applies to

The game is free on Android, iPhone and iPad. It shows ads from Google AdMob and offers optional purchases: a one-time "Remove ads" and packs of in-game coins. Everything below applies to all versions.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, or any account. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect personal information ourselves.

The game stores your career progress, coins, unlocked colours and masks, and your sound and graphics settings on your device only. They are deleted when you uninstall the game.

Match commentary may be spoken with your device's built-in text-to-speech. This happens on your device; no audio or text is sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking)Advertising, frequency capping, fraud prevention
IP address and approximate location derived from itAdvertising, fraud prevention
Ad interactions, app diagnostics and performance dataAdvertising, analytics

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice any time with the "Privacy choices" link on the main menu.
  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks before tracking you across other apps. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared, and you can change this any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • Remove ads: the one-time "Remove ads" purchase turns off the ads shown between matches. Optional ads you choose to watch for a reward (such as double coins) stay available.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment details. We only receive confirmation that the purchase was made. Coins are an in-game currency with no real-world value; they cannot be exchanged for money.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and deletion

Because we do not hold personal data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Uninstalling the game removes all data it stored on your device. Requests about data held by Google can be made through Google's privacy tools.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this address.

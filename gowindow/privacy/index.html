GoWindow Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Developer: CraftByte4Fun · Contact: craftbyte4fun@gmail.com

GoWindow ("the app") finds the best time to run, and optionally to walk your dog, based on the local weather forecast and, if you allow it, the free time in your calendar. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

The short version

  • We do not run servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Your calendar is read on your iPhone only. Event details never leave your device.
  • Your location is used only to get your local forecast from Apple Weather.
  • Everything the app saves stays on your iPhone and is deleted when you delete the app.

What the app uses

InformationHow it is usedWhere it goes
Location (while using the app) To get the hourly forecast and weather alerts for where you are. Sent to Apple's WeatherKit service to fetch the forecast. Not sent to us. The last forecast location is saved on your iPhone so the app and its widgets can refresh.
Calendar (optional) To find the times you are free. The app reads only when events start and end, and whether they are all-day, cancelled or marked as free. It does not read or store event titles, notes, attendees or locations. Stays on your iPhone.
Your settings and dog profile To tailor the plan: which activities you plan for, your dog's size, age and coat, your heat and cold tolerances, usual times and alert preferences. Stays on your iPhone.
Notifications (optional) A reminder before your best free window. Scheduled on your iPhone. No push server is involved.

Apple Weather

Forecasts come from Apple's WeatherKit. Apple's handling of the request is described in the Apple Privacy Policy. Weather data sources are listed at Apple Weather data sources.

Purchases

GoWindow Pro subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details or Apple ID. The app only receives confirmation from Apple that Pro is active. You can manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Your choices

  • You can turn off Location, Calendar or Notifications access any time in iOS Settings › GoWindow. Without calendar access, the app uses the usual times you set instead.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it saved on your iPhone.

Health and safety

GoWindow's scores, pavement temperature estimates and gear tips are general guidance based on weather forecasts, not medical or veterinary advice. Forecasts can be wrong. Always check conditions yourself, especially pavement temperature with the back of your hand before walking your dog.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Data retention and deletion

Because we do not hold any personal data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this address.

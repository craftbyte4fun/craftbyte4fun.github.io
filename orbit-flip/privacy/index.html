Orbit Flip Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026 · Developer: CraftByte4Fun · Contact: craftbyte4fun@gmail.com

Orbit Flip ("the game") is a single-player game. This policy explains what information is handled when you play it.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, or any account. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect personal information ourselves.

The game stores your best score and your sound and vibration settings on your device only. They are deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking)Advertising, frequency capping, fraud prevention
IP address and approximate location derived from itAdvertising, fraud prevention
Ad interactions, app diagnostics and performance dataAdvertising, analytics

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy choices.
  • iOS tracking: the game asks for permission before using your device's advertising identifier. You can change this in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • Remove ads: the one-time "Remove ads" purchase turns off interstitial ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment details. We only receive confirmation that the purchase was made.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and deletion

Because we do not hold personal data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Uninstalling the game removes all data it stored on your device. Requests about data held by Google can be made through Google's privacy tools.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this address.
